## Changes

Slide 4 replaces the stiff mechanism table with an original-model view, editable sun rays, pedestrian, airflow arrows and conditional plant-water arrows. Shade is modelled; airflow and evapotranspiration remain qualitative mechanisms. The audit logic table remains in the companion spreadsheet.

Slide 10 explains the source proposal: two Boston ivy plus one Rangoon creeper, with trumpet creeper as an alternative. Botanical names and nursery identity remain subject to checking; site winter conditions may rule out the proposed companion.

Slide 11 illustrates planting/training, reaching the crown, lateral spreading and maintaining shade. Images reuse the supplied spatial-cover scenarios; they are not an actual biological simulation or successive measured years. An assumed-rate example uses a 6.9m route and 1.5m initial shoot: 1/2/3m extension per growing season gives 6/3/2 seasons to the route end. These rates have no verified species-specific basis. This is not time to full cover or a commitment to opening.
